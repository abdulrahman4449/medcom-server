Deleting your MEDCOM Dispatch account and data

App: MEDCOM Dispatch

MEDCOM Dispatch accounts are issued by the ambulance service that employs you — you cannot create one yourself, and you cannot delete one from inside the app. Deletion is handled by that service's administrator so that a departing member of staff cannot remove operational records the service is legally required to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email [CONTACT EMAIL ADDRESS] from your work address, or contact your service's MEDCOM administrator directly.
  2. Give your name and employee ID. You do not need to give a reason.
  3. The administrator removes the account from the board's account list.

Requests are normally actioned within 30 days. You will be told when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is not

DeletedKept
Your account: employee ID, display name, role, and the stored password hash. Once removed you can no longer sign in, and your name no longer appears in the roster or on the crew lists. Operational records of calls that were run — times, vehicles, outcomes, and the shift logs already submitted. These are the ambulance service's clinical and operational record, kept under its records-retention rules, and they may name the crew who attended. They are not deleted on request.

This is the same position as a paper run sheet: the employee record can be closed, but the record of the calls that were attended stays in the service's archive. If you believe a specific record about you is inaccurate, ask your administrator to correct it rather than delete it.

Data held on your own device

Signing out clears your session from the device. To remove everything the app has kept locally — preferences, and anything queued while offline — clear the site data for this app in your browser or device settings, or uninstall the app.
